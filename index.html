How to download a Facebook Reel

  1. Open the Reel in the Facebook app or browser, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link in the box above.
  3. Tap Get video, then choose HD or SD to save the file.

Questions

Does it work for private videos?

No. Only public Reels and videos can be downloaded. If the video is private or limited to friends, the tool will show an error.

Where is the video saved?

On a phone it goes to your Downloads folder or the browser's download list. On a computer it goes to the Downloads folder.

Which links are supported?

Links from facebook.com, m.facebook.com and fb.watch, including Reels and normal public videos.

Can I reuse any video I download?

Download only your own videos or videos you have permission to save. Reels belong to their creators.
